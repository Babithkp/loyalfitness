The story
behind Loyal

Loyal Fitness is a neighbourhood strength gym in Kushalnagar, Coorg, for people who want to train seriously and stay consistent.

Inside the gymLoyal Fitness

Loyalty to the work is what changes your body.

Loyal Fitness is built around a simple idea: show up, train with good form, and keep going. The floor is set up for strength, muscle building and steady fat loss.

You will find us on the 3rd floor of Abhi Gowda Complex. Look for the red signboard on Old Market Road.

Where exactly is Loyal Fitness gym in Kushalnagar?+

Loyal Fitness is on the 3rd floor of Abhi Gowda Complex, Old Market Road, opposite the Kodava cemetery in Kushalnagar, Karnataka 571234. It is near the Ganapathi Temple and the bus stand. Look for the red Loyal Fitness signboard.

What are the gym timings?+

Loyal Fitness is open in the morning and evening. Call or WhatsApp +91 98865 43872 and we will share the current timings.

How much does a membership cost?+

Call or WhatsApp +91 98865 43872 and we will share the current membership fees. You can visit and see the gym before you pay anything.

What training programs does Loyal Fitness offer?+

Loyal Fitness offers four programs: strength training with barbells and dumbbells, muscle gain with structured weekly splits, fat loss combining weights and conditioning, and a beginner program that teaches gym basics and proper form.

Is there a personal trainer at the gym?+

Yes. Head trainer Brijesh Kumar guides members on form, sets up a routine that matches their goal and tracks their progress in strength, muscle building and fat loss.

I have never been to a gym. Can I join?+

Yes. Beginners are welcome. The trainer will help you with basic form and a simple routine to start safely.

Can I visit before I join?+

Of course. Message us, come over, and see the equipment and the space first.

What should I bring on day one?+

Comfortable workout clothes, clean indoor shoes, a towel and a water bottle.
